# Customized Sales Pitch Scripts Using Jordan Belfort's Straight Line Selling Method

## Distributor Sales Pitch

### Step 1: Take Control in the First 4 Seconds
"Hello, my name is [Your Name] from [Your Company Name]. We're the leading specialized rain gutter supply business in Eastern Pennsylvania, and I'm reaching out specifically to distributors like yourself who might benefit from our unique product line. Do you have a moment to discuss how we might complement your current offerings?"

### Step 2: Build Rapport and Gather Intelligence
"Before I get into specifics, I'd like to understand your current gutter product offerings. What types of gutter materials and accessories do you currently distribute to your customers? And what are some of the challenges you face in keeping a comprehensive inventory of gutter products?"

### Step 3: Transition to the Presentation
"Based on what you've shared, I can see how our specialized gutter supply services could be valuable to your business. Let me explain why distributors like yourself choose to partner with us..."

### Step 4: Present the Three 10s
**Product (10/10):** "Unlike general building material distributors, we specialize exclusively in rain gutters, downspouts, and accessories. This specialization allows us to offer the most comprehensive selection of gutter products in the region, with over 25 color options and every conceivable fitting and accessory. Our inventory depth means your customers never have to wait for special orders."

**You as the Salesperson (10/10):** "I've been in the gutter supply business for [X years] and understand the unique challenges distributors face with inventory management and technical support. I personally work with each of our distributor partners to ensure you have exactly what you need when you need it."

**Company (10/10):** "Our company was founded specifically to address the gaps in the gutter supply chain. We maintain the largest dedicated gutter inventory in Eastern PA, with same-day or next-day availability on all standard items. We've built our reputation on reliability and technical expertise that general distributors simply can't match."

### Step 5: Create Future Pacing
"Imagine having a specialized partner who handles all your gutter supply needs, allowing you to focus on your core product lines while still meeting all your customers' gutter requirements. Picture your customers being impressed by your ability to provide even specialty gutter items without delays. That's the relationship we want to build with you."

### Step 6: Address Objections
**For "We already have suppliers":** "I understand you have existing relationships. Many of our best distributor partners started by using us as a backup for specialty items their primary suppliers couldn't provide quickly. Over time, they found our specialized focus actually complemented their existing supply chain rather than competing with it."

**For "Your prices might be higher":** "While we're not always the cheapest option for standard items, our specialized focus means we're often more competitive on specialty products. More importantly, our reliability and technical support often save you money in the long run by preventing costly mistakes and delays."

### Step 7: Close with Confidence
"Based on what we've discussed, I'd like to propose starting with a trial partnership where we supply you with specialty gutter products that complement your existing inventory. This allows you to test our service with minimal risk. Could we schedule a brief meeting next week to discuss specific products that might fit this arrangement? I'm also happy to provide our distributor catalog and pricing structure for your review."
